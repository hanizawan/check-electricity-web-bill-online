Check Electricity Web Bill Online is an independent guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to PITC (Power Information Technology Company), any electricity distribution company (DISCO), K-Electric, or the Government of Pakistan.

Where Your Bill Comes From

All bill data — units consumed, amounts, taxes and due dates — comes from the official PITC Web Bill service at bill.pitc.com.pk. This website only helps you reach the correct official page. We do not generate, store or alter any bill.

Verify Before You Pay

Always verify the payable amount and due date on the official bill that opens on the PITC website before paying. If anything on your bill looks wrong, do not rely on this guide — contact your distribution company or call the electricity helpline 118.

Printed Duplicate Bills

Printed copies of bills from the official PITC website are generally accepted for payment at bank branches and through banking channels, but acceptance is decided by the bank or payment service, not by us.
