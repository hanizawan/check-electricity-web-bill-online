Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about checking your bill online.

Is this service free?

Yes. Checking and printing your duplicate bill is completely free. You only pay your actual electricity bill amount when you pay it at a bank, through a banking app, or through a mobile wallet.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. There is no registration and no login. You only need the 14-digit reference number or the 10-digit customer ID printed on any old electricity bill.

Where do I find my reference number?

Your reference number is printed at the top of every electricity bill — it is 14 digits long. Your customer ID is a different 10-digit number, also printed on the bill. Neither of them is the meter number stamped on your meter.

Can I pay with a printed duplicate bill?

Yes. The bill you view and print from the official PITC page works as a duplicate bill. You can pay it at any bank branch, through your banking app, or through a mobile wallet using the same reference number.

Is this the official website?

No. Check Electricity Web Bill Online is an independent guide, not an official website. When you check your bill here, it opens on the official PITC website (bill.pitc.com.pk) — the same official source your electricity company uses.
