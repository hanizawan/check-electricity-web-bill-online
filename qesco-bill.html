QESCO Bill Check Online — View & Print Duplicate Bill

Quetta Electric Supply Company (Quetta and Balochistan) — check your latest electricity bill by reference number or customer ID. Your bill opens on the official PITC website.

Reference Number = the 14-digit number printed at the top of your bill.

Your bill will open directly on the official PITC website (bill.pitc.com.pk) in a new tab. We do not store your number.

How to Check Your QESCO Bill

  1. Find your 14-digit reference number or 10-digit customer ID on any old QESCO bill.
  2. Select Reference Number or Customer ID in the checker above (for a reference number, also pick Domestic or Industrial) and type the number — spaces and dashes are removed automatically.
  3. Press View My Bill. Your latest bill opens in a new tab on the official PITC website.
  4. Print the bill or save it as a PDF. The printed copy works as a duplicate bill for payment at any bank branch, banking app or mobile wallet.

Where to Find Your Reference Number

Your reference number is printed at the top of every QESCO electricity bill, near your name and address — it is exactly 14 digits long. The customer ID is a separate 10-digit number also printed on the bill. Neither number is the meter number stamped on your electricity meter, so always copy the number from an old bill. If you cannot find an old bill, call the electricity helpline 118 for help.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check my QESCO bill online?

Enter your 14-digit reference number (or 10-digit customer ID) in the checker above and press View My Bill. Your latest QESCO bill opens on the official PITC website, where you can view, download and print it.

I lost my reference number. What can I do?

Use the 10-digit customer ID printed on any old bill instead — select Customer ID in the checker above. If you have no old bill at all, call the electricity helpline 118 or visit your nearest DISCO office with your meter number to get your reference number.

Can I pay my QESCO bill on this website?

No — this is a bill viewing and printing guide only. After printing your duplicate bill from the official PITC page, pay it at any bank branch, through your banking app, or through a mobile wallet. For bill complaints, call the helpline 118.
